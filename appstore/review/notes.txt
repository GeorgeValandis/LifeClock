LifeClock does not require a user account or login.

The app's core flow is:
1. Complete onboarding by entering a birth date, life expectancy, display unit, and theme.
2. Review the live time counter and optional life grid visualization.
3. Open Settings to adjust appearance and life profile details.
4. Open the Lifetime paywall from Settings to test the one-time unlock flow.

RevenueCat is used for the lifetime non-consumable product:
- Product ID: com.GA.LifeClock.lifetime
- Entitlement ID: premium
- Offering ID: default

If the review build runs without StoreKit connectivity, the paywall should still render and the app remains fully navigable. No demo account is required.
